OUR PROJECT

What is Nepal Live Rates?

📊

A Real-Time Financial Data Platform

Nepal Live Rates is a web project focused on displaying important financial and market information related to Nepal.

The platform currently focuses on foreign exchange rates published by Nepal Rastra Bank and presents the information through a modern, responsive and easy-to-understand interface.
